Digital Twin

Digital Twin is a digital model of an intended or actual real-world physical product, system, or process that serves as a digital counterpart of it for purposes like simulation, integration, testing, monitoring, and maintenance.

Why Digital Twins?

Digital twin technology is an exciting change in how people engage with technology and physical infrastructure, which is why I picked it. Previously impossible possibilities are made possible by the idea that a complicated machine, like a jet engine or an entire wind farm, can exist simultaneously in the real world and as a live-updating virtual model. Modern engineering is totally transformed by the ability to do stress testing, predict failures, and maximize energy efficiency in a risk-free digital environment.

Also, digital twins are quickly spreading outside of industrial manufacturing into areas like urban planning and personalized health care. I became comfortable knowing that this field will lead to significant technological advancements in the upcoming decades after learning how virtual cardiovascular models assist surgeons in simulating procedures or how smart cities control traffic flow.
