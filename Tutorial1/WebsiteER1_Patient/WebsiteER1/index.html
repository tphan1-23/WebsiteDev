Emergency Guide to Surviving Your First HTML Page

Don't panic. Your webpage can probably be saved.

Common Symptoms of HTML Trouble

Your webpage may need attention if you notice any of these symptoms:

  • Your image has mysteriously disappeared.
  • Your link goes absolutely nowhere.
  • Your list looks a little strange.
  • The browser is doing something you definitely did not ask it to do.

Before You Call for Help

Browsers can be surprisingly forgiving. A page may appear to work even when the HTML contains errors.

When something goes wrong, inspect your code carefully. Check your document structure, elements, attributes, file paths, and links.
