About Webster

Webster is the unofficial mascot of the Website ER. He enjoys cookies, clean code, and websites that behave exactly the way they are supposed to.

Unfortunately, Webster has learned that webpages do not always cooperate. When something breaks, he prefers a calm diagnosis, a careful repair, and at least one cookie afterward.
