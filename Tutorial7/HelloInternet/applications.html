Digital Twin Applications

Digital twins are used in various places. This page compares real-world uses across several sectors, showing what each twin models, which live data keeps it up to date, and the main benefit it delivers.

Digital Twins Across Industries

Digital twin use cases by sector, with the data they rely on and the benefit they provide
Sector Use Case What the Twin Models Live Data Inputs Main Benefit
Industry and Energy Jet engine monitoring Engine temperature, pressure, and wear Onboard sensors Predicts failures before they happen
Wind farm optimization Turbines, blade angles, and wind patterns Weather and turbine output data Maximizes energy efficiency
Manufacturing line Machines, conveyors, and workflow timing Machine sensors and production logs Finds bottlenecks without halting production
Cities and Buildings Smart city traffic Roads, intersections, and vehicle flow Traffic cameras and signal data Reduces congestion
Urban planning Buildings, utilities, and population growth Maps, utility meters, and census data Tests new developments before construction
Building energy use Heating, cooling, and occupancy Thermostats and occupancy sensors Lowers energy costs
Health Care Cardiovascular model A patient's heart and blood flow Medical scans and heart monitors Lets surgeons rehearse procedures
Personalized treatment A patient's body response to medication Health records and wearable devices Tailors care to the individual
